At launch: bridge in, use it, withdraw

  1. Have ETH on Robinhood Chain. That is all you need: the bridge swaps it to GME for you. Holding GME already? You can deposit it directly.
  2. Connect. Open the bridge page and connect a wallet.
  3. Bridge. Enter an ETH amount. The page shows a live Uniswap quote; one transaction swaps it to GME and deposits it. It arrives on GME CHAIN as gas, usually within a minute.
  4. Use it. Send, deploy contracts, or trade on the exchange. A transfer costs a fraction of a cent.
  5. Withdraw. Start a withdrawal on the bridge page. After about 7 days the countdown ends; press Claim to get your GME back on Robinhood Chain, then Convert to ETH if you want ETH.
Real funds

One operator will run the sequencer, batch poster and validator at first, GME can be paused by its issuer, and the contracts have not had an external audit yet. Start with amounts you are comfortable with. Withdrawals cannot be sped up or cancelled once started.

What is GME CHAIN

GME CHAIN is an independent blockchain, a Layer 3 built with Arbitrum Orbit. It runs on top of Robinhood Chain, which itself settles to Ethereum. It behaves like any EVM chain: the same wallets, the same Solidity contracts, the same tools.

The one thing that makes it different is the token you pay gas with. On Ethereum you pay in ETH. On GME CHAIN every fee is paid in GME, the Robinhood stock token that tracks GameStop Corp. shares (NYSE: GME).

In one line

A fast, low-cost EVM chain where the gas token is GameStop's ticker.

Independent

GME CHAIN is not made, endorsed or sponsored by GameStop Corp., Robinhood Markets, Inc. or Offchain Labs. "GME" is the ticker of the token used as gas.

Why gas is paid in GME

GME is one of the most widely followed stocks among retail traders. A chain whose unit of account is GME makes that the currency of everything on it: every balance, every fee and every msg.value. You do not need to hold GME to start, either: bring ETH and the bridge swaps it for you.

  • One asset, two jobs. If you already hold GME, you can use the same balance to pay for transactions instead of selling it for ETH first.
  • Tiny fees either way. A transfer costs about 28,400 gas at 0.1 gwei, well under a cent at today's GME price (about $0.0001 at $27).
  • Real, not a wrapper. The gas is the GME Stock Token itself, locked in the bridge on Robinhood Chain one to one. There is no synthetic version and no separate "gas coin".

Paying gas in GME does not give you shares, votes or any rights in GameStop. The GME Stock Token gives price exposure only, under its issuer's terms (see About the GME Stock Token).

How it works

  1. You sign. Your wallet sends a transaction to GME CHAIN and pays gas in GME.
  2. The sequencer orders it into a block within a second or two.
  3. A batch lands on Robinhood Chain. Blocks are compressed and posted to the sequencer inbox. The batch poster pays ETH for that and is repaid in GME.
  4. A validator vouches for it. A bonded validator posts the resulting state using BoLD fraud proofs. Anyone can challenge a wrong claim during the challenge window: about 7 days on mainnet, about 30 minutes on testnet. Counting the batch and the claim itself, a testnet withdrawal can be claimed 45 to 60 minutes after it starts.
  5. Ethereum has the final say. Robinhood Chain settles to Ethereum, so GME CHAIN shares its root of trust with every Arbitrum chain.

How fees are priced

Posting batches costs ETH on Robinhood Chain, but users pay in GME. A fee pricer contract converts between them using Chainlink's ETH/USD feed and the GME market price. A keeper updates the GME price from the 30-minute time-weighted average of the deepest GME pool on Robinhood Chain (Uniswap v3 GME/USDG), checked against a second pool, with guards for low liquidity, stale data, large jumps, paused trading and corporate actions, and freezes rather than guessing when markets are closed or unusual.

Bridging with ETH or GME

Deposit with ETH: Robinhood Chain → GME CHAIN

  1. Connect a regular wallet (an EOA) and choose Pay with ETH.
  2. Enter an amount. The page quotes every Uniswap v3 route between WETH and GME on Robinhood Chain and shows what will arrive.
  3. Confirm one transaction. The GmeGasRouter contract wraps your ETH, swaps it to GME (reverting if the price moved more than 1%), locks the GME in the GME CHAIN bridge and credits it to your address as gas, usually within a minute.

Deposit with GME

  1. Choose Pay with GME, approve the GME CHAIN inbox (first time only) and deposit. The same amount arrives as gas.

Withdrawal: GME CHAIN → Robinhood Chain → ETH

  1. Start the withdrawal on GME CHAIN. Your GME is burned there and a message is created. Keep a little GME for this transaction's gas.
  2. Wait for the challenge window: about 7 days on mainnet (50,400 Ethereum blocks). The bridge page shows a countdown. This is the security model, not a delay we can shorten.
  3. Claim on Robinhood Chain from the bridge page. Your GME is released from the bridge to you.
  4. Want ETH? Convert to ETH on the same page swaps it through the GmeGasRouter in one transaction, with the same 1% price limit.
The router holds nothing

GmeGasRouter has no owner, no fee and no upgrade. Each call spends its whole input and sends every output on in the same transaction, and the deposit ticket names you as both receiver and refund address, so the GME can only end up with you. Source: GmeGasRouter.sol.

By hand

Paying with ETH calls depositEth(path, minGmeOut, to, gasLimit, maxFeePerGas) on the GmeGasRouter. Paying with GME calls approve and createRetryableTicket(…) on the inbox. Withdrawing calls withdrawEth(destination) on ArbSys and executeTransaction(…) on the outbox; converting calls swapGmeToEth(amountIn, path, minEthOut, to).

The exchange

The GME CHAIN exchange runs the Uniswap V2 contracts, deployed unmodified from Uniswap's published bytecode, with Wrapped GME (WGME) as the base asset. Anyone can create a pool and add liquidity. Each trade pays the standard 0.3% fee to the pool's liquidity providers.

  • GME and WGME are the same asset. Wrapping is one to one and always reversible.
  • Pools start empty. The first deposit into a pool sets its starting price.
  • Check addresses. Anyone can create a token with any name. Trade only tokens whose contract you have verified.
Status

The exchange is deployed with mainnet and opens on the swap page at launch. Pools are created by their liquidity providers.

Trust model and risks

  • One operator at launch. A single operator runs the sequencer, the batch poster and the validator. That is normal for a new Arbitrum chain and the main thing you are trusting.
  • Censorship has a way out. If the sequencer ignores you, you can push a transaction through the delayed inbox on Robinhood Chain; it must be included after 4 days (28,800 Ethereum blocks).
  • Wrong state can be challenged. The validator posts a bond and its claims can be disputed with BoLD fraud proofs during the challenge window (~7 days on mainnet, ~30 minutes on testnet).
  • Upgrades are possible. The chain's contracts can be upgraded by its owner through the upgrade executor. There is no timelock or multisig yet.
  • The gas token has an issuer. Robinhood can pause, block, burn and upgrade GME (see About the GME Stock Token).
  • Beta software. The bridge and swap pages and the GmeGasRouter are new and not externally audited; they sit over audited Arbitrum and Uniswap contracts. Use amounts you can afford to have delayed.
  • Price risk. GME tracks a share price. Your gas balance's dollar value moves with it.

How the code is checked

  • Open source. Every contract, script and page is on GitHub (MIT).
  • Automatic tests. Every change runs the contract tests (unit, fuzz, and fork tests against Robinhood Chain mainnet) and a check that no keys are committed: CI results.
  • On-chain code = this code. CI compiles the sources and compares them byte for byte with every contract deployed on mainnet; anyone can run the same check. Every contract, operator wallet, owner power and fee flow is listed in TRANSPARENCY.md.
  • Not an audit. These are automatic checks. The contracts have not had an external audit yet.

FAQ

Do I need ETH on GME CHAIN?

No. Gas on GME CHAIN is GME. You only need a little ETH on Robinhood Chain to make the deposit and, later, to claim a withdrawal.

How long does withdrawing take?

About 7 days on mainnet and about an hour on testnet. Withdrawals wait for the validator's claim to survive the challenge window, the same rule as Arbitrum One; testnet uses a short window so the whole flow can be tried. The bridge page shows a countdown. Deposits do not wait.

Can I bridge from a Safe or another smart wallet?

Not through the bridge page. Deposits from contracts are credited to an aliased address on GME CHAIN. Use a regular wallet.

Does holding the GME CHAIN token give me anything at GameStop?

No. $GMEC is for GME CHAIN only; its uses are announced together with its contract. It has no connection to GameStop.

What happens if GME is paused by its issuer?

Transfers on Robinhood Chain stop, so deposits and claims stop too. GME already on GME CHAIN keeps working as gas there.

Is there a block explorer?

Yes. The GME CHAIN Explorer shows blocks, transactions and addresses live from the chain, for mainnet and testnet.

Glossary

Layer 3 (L3)
A chain that settles to a Layer 2. GME CHAIN settles to Robinhood Chain, which settles to Ethereum.
Sequencer
The service that orders transactions into blocks.
Batch poster
Posts compressed blocks to the parent chain so anyone can rebuild the chain.
Validator / BoLD
Posts claims about the chain's state with a bond; BoLD is Arbitrum's dispute protocol for challenging wrong claims.
Challenge window
The time a claim must survive before withdrawals it covers can be claimed: ~7 days on mainnet, ~30 minutes on testnet.
Fee token pricer
The contract that converts parent-chain costs (ETH) into GME using Chainlink ETH/USD and the GME price.
WGME
Wrapped GME, the ERC-20 form of GME used by the exchange. Always one to one.
