January 2021 showed what retail players can do with one ticker. GME CHAIN gives that ticker a job: every transaction here is paid in GME, Robinhood's GameStop stock token. Hold it, spend it, trade it.

    One-button combo

    ETH in, GME gas out: swap and bridge in a single transaction, 1% price limit.

  • ★0

    No grind

    New coins to buy first. Bring ETH, leave with ETH; GME is the only token in the loop.

  • ★$0

    No house cut

    Bridge fee. The router has no owner and keeps nothing; you pay pool fees only.

Ethereum is the final boss.

Blocks are batched to Robinhood Chain, which settles to Ethereum. A bonded validator posts the chain's state there, and a wrong claim can be disputed with BoLD fraud proofs during the one-day window. At launch only our allowlisted validator can post or dispute claims; the allowlist opens later.

Warning

Read before you play

Built in

  • Verified code. Every contract we deployed matches its published source byte for byte, and anyone can re-run the check.
  • The ETH bridge holds nothing. GmeGasRouter has no owner and no fee. Each call spends its whole input, and the deposit can only land at your address.
  • Exact approvals. The site never asks for unlimited token approvals.
  • A real safety window. Withdrawals wait about a day so a wrong state claim can be challenged.

Know first

  • One operator at launch runs the sequencer, the batch poster and the only allowlisted validator. It can delay transactions, and until the allowlist opens, withdrawals rely on it posting honest state. Every batch is public on Robinhood Chain, so anyone can check it.
  • GME has an issuer. Robinhood can pause GME, block addresses (including the bridge), burn balances and upgrade the token.
  • Not for everyone. The GME token is not available to US persons and is restricted in some other places.
  • New software. No external audit yet. Start with amounts you're comfortable with.

1-7Loading tips

Stuck? Tips

The manual walks through every step.

01I'm new. Where do I start?

Open the bridge, connect a wallet, type an amount of ETH and confirm one transaction. Your GME arrives as gas on GME CHAIN in about a minute. Before mainnet launch you can try the whole flow on testnet for free.

02Do I need to own GME first?

No. Pay with ETH and the bridge buys the GME for you on Uniswap v3. If you already hold GME on Robinhood Chain, you can deposit it directly instead.

03What does it cost?

A transfer on GME CHAIN costs a fraction of a cent. Bridging in costs normal Robinhood Chain gas plus the Uniswap pool fee, which is already inside the quote you see. Withdrawing costs GME CHAIN gas to start (a fraction of a cent) and Robinhood Chain gas to claim. The bridge itself takes no fee.

04How long do deposits and withdrawals take?

Deposits arrive in about a minute. Withdrawals take about a day: that's the challenge window that keeps the bridge safe. The bridge page shows a countdown and a Claim button, then lets you convert back to ETH.

05Which wallets work?

MetaMask, Rabby, Coinbase Wallet, OKX, Phantom, Trust, Bitget, Binance Wallet, Rainbow, Ledger, and any wallet that connects through WalletConnect.

06Is GME CHAIN made by GameStop or Robinhood?

No. GME CHAIN is independent and not affiliated with, endorsed or sponsored by GameStop Corp., Robinhood Markets, Inc. or Offchain Labs. "GME" is the ticker of the token used as gas.

07What is $GMEC?

$GMEC will be GME CHAIN's own token. It hasn't launched yet, so any token called GMEC you see today isn't ours. Its contract will be published on this site, and only here. Gas always stays GME.
