You sign a message to prove the wallet is yours (free, no transaction). The credits used are recorded on chain, and the gas arrives at the same address on GME CHAIN.

How credits work

  1. Stake. Approve once, then stake any amount. Credits start right away.
  2. Earn. Credits are token-days: 100 staked for 3 days is 300. They keep counting every second.
  3. Use. Claim gas on GME CHAIN with your credits, once a day. Each use is recorded on chain.
  4. Leave any time. Unstaking is never blocked, and earned credits are kept.

What credits are not

Credits are not a token. They cannot be transferred, sold or traded, and they pay no yield or reward. They are only a right to use gas on GME CHAIN.
